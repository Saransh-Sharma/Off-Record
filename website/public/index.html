Weekly Reflection

A private look back at what shaped your week.

When you have written enough, OffRecord gathers the week into a calm report you can read on Sunday night or whenever you are ready.

  • See what kept coming up. Themes, small wins, friction points, and questions help you notice the week without over-reading it.
  • Choose which entries are included. Open the source list, remove an entry from the reflection, or add it back later.
  • Save one takeaway for next week. Keep the line you want to remember, then find it again in history.

Privacy by design

Your journal is not a dataset. It is yours.

Most AI journal apps ask you to send private writing away. OffRecord keeps your journal, reflections, and quick captures with you.

  • No account required. Start journaling without an email address, login, or profile.
  • No analytics or ad tracking. The app is built without third-party analytics, advertising, or telemetry SDKs.
  • Private reflections. Weekly Reflection is made from your own entries and stays in the app.
  • Optional iCloud sync. Sync through your personal Apple account only when you choose it.
  • Private Apple Watch capture. Save quick thoughts on your wrist without showing journal text on the watch face.
